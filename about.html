About

Hi, I'm Dylan.

Full-stack developer. I like small, sharp tools and shipping things that work.

I'm a developer who enjoys the whole journey of a product — figuring out the problem, shaping a clean interface, and getting the details right on the way out the door. I care a lot about performance, accessibility, and code that's easy to pick up later.

Most of my work lives somewhere between the browser and the server: thoughtful front-ends, pragmatic APIs, and the glue in between. When I'm not building, I'm usually reading about systems design or tinkering with a side project.

How I work

  • Start from the user — ship the smallest thing that solves the real problem.
  • Keep it readable — the next person to open the file is probably future me.
  • Measure, don't guess — performance and accessibility are features, not afterthoughts.
